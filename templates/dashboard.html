Money in vs. money out — HSBC & Halifax current accounts

This is gross cash movement through the two bank accounts that actually hold money — every credit and every debit, month by month (salary, transfers, direct debits, card settlements, ATM withdrawals, all of it). It's a different lens from the category chart above: that one shows categorised spend; this one shows the full in/out of the accounts themselves. Credit-card purchases don't appear here directly — only the current-account payment that later settles the card bill does.

Direct Debits by month — HSBC & Halifax current accounts

Every transaction the bank itself flags as a Direct Debit (type code "DD" on the statement), stacked by account. These are the recurring, automatic payments — loans, utilities, subscriptions, insurance — that leave the account without you actively initiating them each time.

ATM & cash withdrawals by month

Every withdrawal the bank's own statement codes as a cash machine transaction (type "ATM" on HSBC, "CPT" on Halifax) — identified from the statement's own transaction-type code, not by guessing from the location name, so a cash machine inside a Tesco or at a Shell garage is correctly counted here rather than under Groceries or Fuel.

Monthly spend by card & bank

Every categorised, de-duplicated transaction total, month by month, split by which physical card or account it was actually spent on — two debit cards (HSBC and Halifax current accounts) and two credit cards (HSBC and Barclaycard/Amazon). Monthly deep dive — total spend by payee, month by month

One panel per month. Every transaction is grouped by payee/merchant (the same de-duplicated name used elsewhere in this dashboard) and summed, so a payee charged 5 times in a month appears once, ranked by its total for that month — biggest first, coloured by category. This answers "how much did I actually spend on each payee this month", rather than listing every individual line item separately.
